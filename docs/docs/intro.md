# Overview

**Karter** was born with the mission of creating the best **Open Source** vehicle maintenance app, respecting privacy and donating **20% of affiliate revenue** to social causes.

> **Latest release:** [v2026.06.1-alpha](https://github.com/abrahdev/karter/releases/tag/v2026.06.1-alpha)

## Why Open Source?

* **Privacy:** Your mechanical and location data should not be commodified.

* **Transparency:** Anyone can audit how we calculate maintenance costs.

## The 20% Promise 🎗️
We donate 20% of the revenue generated through affiliate links (parts, tools) to NGOs chosen by the community.
