# Karter

**Karter** is a privacy-first, **open source** vehicle maintenance tracker built
with Flutter. It was born with the mission of creating the best vehicle
maintenance app while respecting your privacy and donating **20% of affiliate
revenue** to social causes.

> **Latest release:** [<AppVersion />](https://github.com/abrahdev/karter/releases/tag/<AppVersion />)

## Features

- **Multi-vehicle management** — add, edit, delete vehicles with type, VIN, and plate
- **Fuel tracking** — log fill-ups with automatic economy calculations (L/100km, MPG, km/L)
- **Maintenance log** — track repairs, part replacements, and service history
- **Service intervals** — set reminders based on distance or time, seeded from community templates
- **Spare parts** — catalog and track replacement parts
- **Odometer tracking** — log readings in km or mi
- **Dashboard & charts** — statistics over your vehicles' data
- **JSON export / import** — full data backup, portable between devices
- **Community templates** — maintenance schedules contributed per make/model, synced from an online repo
- **OBD-II integration** — ELM327 support (coming soon)
- **Material Design 3** — dynamic colors and dark mode
- **100% offline** — no accounts, no telemetry, no tracking

## Why Karter?

- **Privacy-first:** All data stays on your device in a local SQLite database. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking.
- **Open source:** Anyone can audit the code, verify how costs are calculated, and contribute. Karter is licensed under **AGPL-3.0**.
- **Community-driven data:** Vehicle maintenance templates are a forkable, human-editable JSON package — add your car and open a pull request.

## The 20% Promise 🎗️

We donate **20% of the revenue** generated through affiliate links (parts, tools) to NGOs chosen by the community.

## Getting started

- **Download** the latest APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/abrahdev/karter/releases) (or add the repo as an app source in [Obtainium](https://obtanium.org)).
