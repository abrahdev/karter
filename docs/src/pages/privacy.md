# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 3, 2026_

## Data Collection

Karter does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data entered by you (vehicles, fuel logs, maintenance records) is stored **exclusively on your device** using a local SQLite database.

## No Accounts

Karter has no user accounts, registration, or login system. No personal identifiers are requested or stored.

## No Analytics or Crash Reporting

Karter does **not** include any analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, advertising frameworks, or third-party tracking of any kind.

## Data Export

You may export your data at any time via the built-in export feature. Exported files remain under your control and are not transmitted anywhere by the app.

## Third-Party Services

Karter does **not** communicate with any external servers, APIs, or cloud services. The app functions entirely offline.

## Open Source

Karter is open source under the GNU AGPL v3 license. The full source code is available at [github.com/abrahdev/karter](https://github.com/abrahdev/karter) for independent verification of this policy.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be reflected here and communicated through the app's version release notes.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at [github.com/abrahdev/karter/issues](https://github.com/abrahdev/karter/issues).
